Privacy Policy

Turbulence: Cabin Chaos · Effective October 21, 2026

The short version

Turbulence: Cabin Chaos does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game works fully offline.

What stays on your device

To save your game, the app keeps a few things on your device only:

  • the names and looks you give your crew members,
  • your stars, best scores and medals for each flight,
  • your settings, such as sound, haptics and screen shake.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The optional Full Game purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to turbulencesupport@gmail.com.
